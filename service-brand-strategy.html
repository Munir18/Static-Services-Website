Process · 02

How we work.

A four-phase loop. We move fast, but we don't skip steps.

01

Audit

Stakeholder interviews, competitive landscape, audience research. We listen first, write second.

  • Brand interviews (4–8 hrs)
  • Competitor audit
  • Audience research
02

Position

Sharpen the wedge. What you stand for, what you stand against, what you promise.

  • Positioning statement
  • Value proposition matrix
  • Messaging hierarchy
03

Identity

Visual + verbal system. Logo, palette, typography, voice, tone.

  • Logo system
  • Visual identity
  • Voice & tone guide
04

Activate

Brand guidelines, application templates, internal launch.

  • Full brand guidelines (40+ pages)
  • Application templates
  • Team rollout
