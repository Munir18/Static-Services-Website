Small team.

Media K9 is a five-person studio in Islamabad. We started in 2019, building things we wanted to put our name on. Six years later, the rule still holds.

No. 02

We don't pitch. We don't do RFPs. We don't scale by hiring. We scale by saying no to most of the work that finds us — and going deep on the few things we say yes to. It's a quiet kind of confidence. But it's the only one we know how to operate from.

Operating Principles · 03

What we won't compromise on.

Six things we agreed on before we agreed on anything else. They've held up for six years.

01

Senior hands

The people who pitched the work do the work. No bait-and-switch after kickoff.

02

Built for compounding

We make decisions that pay off in quarter three, not week two. The slow path is the fast path.

03

One source of truth

Every metric we report on, we built the dashboard for. Nothing screenshot, nothing massaged.

04

No vanity

We measure what moves the business. Likes, impressions, and reach are footnotes — not the headline.

05

Plain words

If we can't explain a recommendation in two sentences, we don't recommend it.

06

Long enough to matter

Three-month minimum, twelve-month default. Anything shorter and the work hasn't had time to start.
